# HealthPit Terms of Use

Last updated: 3 October 2026

## Provider and purpose

HealthPit is an open-source application and Home Assistant integration for personal health and fitness record management.

Developer: Peter Weber, Taubenheimstr, Stuttgart, Germany.  
Contact: pithealth@pm.me.

You can view personal records on your iPhone and optionally synchronize selected records to a Home Assistant installation you configure.

## Your accounts and authorization

Connect only accounts, records and installations you are authorized to use. Each user controls their own source permissions and Home Assistant credentials. Keep your credentials and access tokens protected.

You may authorize a subset of available data categories. The planned Huawei test integration reads records permitted by Huawei and authorized by you. No Huawei write permission is requested in the current application.

## Huawei test feature

Huawei connectivity is under development. Initial access is subject to Huawei's approval of the test application, which targets up to 100 test users. Huawei decides which data interfaces, permissions and regions are supported.

A selected permission does not guarantee that every device, account or record provides that data. Planned functions are not represented as already completed. Testing and implementation must establish which records can be imported and displayed.

The feature may change as development and verification proceed. Keep your original records or backups when evaluating a test feature.

## Health information

HealthPit presents personal records and source information. It does not diagnose conditions, prescribe treatment or replace professional medical judgment. Provider records can be incomplete, delayed or unavailable.

## Your installation and existing copies

You configure and maintain your own Home Assistant installation, remote access and backups. Different destinations may retain separate copies of your records.

Disconnecting a source stops future access but does not automatically erase records previously imported into HealthPit, Home Assistant or exported backups. Manage those copies using the controls of each destination.
